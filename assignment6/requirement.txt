Design a new feature
Due Tuesday by 4pm Points 10 Submitting a file upload File Types pdf Available after May 26 at 4pm
As a team, design a new feature for your system.  The delivery is a design document that details:

brief description of the new feature and what it aims to do
motivation for why this new feature is valuable to the system
description of relevant current aspects of your system, particularly in terms of important current implementation details
the detailed design of your new feature -- how will it fit with/augments/change the current code base; in other words, what will the new implementation look like; 
a brief description of any limitations of your new feature; and
all intermediate artifacts that the team produces (e.g., whiteboard sketches, diagrams created in a modeling tool, partial design docs, Figma drawings, UML diagrams, Discord discussions -- as applicable) as an appendix to the document.
