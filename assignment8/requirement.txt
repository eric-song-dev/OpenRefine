As a team, implement the design of your new feature and submit a pull request for your system, with the caveat that, if the social context does not allow this, stop short of submitting the pull request and make it only locally.

To Canvas, submit a document that includes:

   1. A link to the pull request

   2. A link to the project guidelines (if available)

   3. A reflection whether the implementation had to deviate from your design and, if so, in what ways and why (what were the strengths of the original design, what were the weaknesses, what did you have to do differently)
